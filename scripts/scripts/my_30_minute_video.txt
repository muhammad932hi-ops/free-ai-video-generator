## PROJECT

Create a **30-minute premium cinematic Pakistani mystery-thriller drama episode** titled:

**JO DIKHTA NAHI — Chapter 01: 3:17 AM**

The entire video must feel like **one professionally produced film episode**, not a collection of disconnected AI clips.

The narrative follows **FAHAD**, a frightened 17-year-old boy who arrives at a police station at exactly **3:17 AM**, covered in rain and with blood on his clothes. He refuses to explain why he is running. A photograph of him is already circulating online accusing him of murder. A small memory card found in his possession eventually exposes a hidden warehouse operation involving working children and a respected businessman. But the deeper mystery is not simply who is guilty. It is why Fahad trusted the police station, what happened to his brother, why the same property reference appears in old and modern records, and why someone inside the system already seems to know what is happening.

The episode must prioritize:

**MYSTERY → SUSPICION → INVESTIGATION → FALSE ASSUMPTION → EVIDENCE → EMOTIONAL REVEAL → SECOND TRUTH → FINAL CLIFFHANGER**

Do not explain the social issue at the beginning.

Do not tell the audience what the story is “about.”

Let the audience discover everything naturally.
